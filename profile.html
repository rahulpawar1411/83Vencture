RAHUL SHARMA

"Capitalized for Vision. Engineered for Scale — Leading strategic investments and operational building across high-potential Indian enterprise sectors."

OFFICIAL PROFILE | FOUNDER & DIRECTOR | EIGHTY THREE VENTURES

As Founder and Director of Eighty Three Ventures, Rahul Sharma leads strategic investment initiatives, corporate governance, and ecosystem growth for portfolio ventures. With over 15 years of active business building experience, he partners with ambitious founders to solve complex operational challenges and scale sustainable businesses.

Prior to founding Eighty Three Ventures, Rahul held key executive leadership roles across logistics, supply-chain technology platforms, and early-stage enterprise ventures, driving multi-million rupee revenue growth and expanding operating networks across India.

"Great businesses are created when strong ideas are supported by the right people, the right strategy and disciplined execution."
— Rahul Sharma, Director

Strategic Investment & Governance

His approach to strategic investment combines long-term patient capital with hands-on operational enablement. Under his stewardship, Eighty Three Ventures has built and backed disruptive ventures across cold-chain logistics (ReeferON) and consumer staples (10on10 Foods).
