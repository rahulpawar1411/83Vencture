Executive Biography

As Founder and Director of Eighty Three Ventures, Rahul Sharma leads strategic investment initiatives, corporate governance, and ecosystem development for portfolio ventures. With over 15 years of active business building experience, he partners with high-potential founders to solve complex operational challenges and scale sustainable businesses.

Prior to founding Eighty Three Ventures, Rahul held key executive leadership roles across logistics, supply-chain platforms, and early-stage enterprise ventures, driving multi-million rupee revenue growth and expanding operating networks across India.
